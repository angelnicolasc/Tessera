# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

## Summary

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. Harassment in
any form is not tolerated. Be excellent to each other; assume positive intent; disagree
about ideas, not people.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (GitHub issues / PRs / Discussions,
project chat channels, in-person events), and applies when an individual is officially
representing the community in public spaces.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
maintainers responsible for enforcement at **nick.dicerutti@gmail.com**. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warnings,
temporary bans, or permanent bans, for behaviours they consider in violation of this Code
of Conduct.

The full text of Contributor Covenant v2.1 governs all unspecified cases.
